Residual Stream Studies

These are studies of a language model's residual stream, the running sum that every layer reads from and writes to. The vectors are simulated but built to behave like real ones: layers add to them, their norm grows with depth, one dimension carries a massive activation, and concepts live in directions. Each piece names its lens, because the numbers only mean something once you decide how to look at them.

truthfulgriefjoydeceptive

The shared lens. Hue is where a vector points in the plane of two concept directions. Saturation is how strongly it points there. Grey means no meaning has been written yet.

Cat. 2How a thought is built

Glazes

26 layer writes to the last token, laid down as translucent washes on paper

The residual stream is a sum. Each layer adds its write to what is already there, the way a painter builds color with thin glazes. Each wash here is one layer's write. It sits where the running total lands, its size is how much that layer wrote, and its tint is what it wrote about. The early washes are small and grey. Color arrives in the middle layers, where the model works out what the sentence means.

Lens position: gallery PCA · tint: valence–honesty of each write

Cat. 3How a thought is built

Loom

Tokens as warp, layers as weft, one cell per token per layer

Each weft row is a layer and each warp column is a token. A cell's color is that token's residual at that layer. Read down a column to watch a word gather meaning with depth. Read along the bottom rows to see the mood spread from the words that carry it to words like “and” and “I”, which carry none of their own. The slider adds a steering vector at layer 8, so only the rows below it change.

Lens valence–honesty, per cell

Cat. 4Geometry of meaning

Three Rivers

Every token's path from layer 0 to layer 26, three prompts, two principal components

The three sentences share words, so their rivers rise from the same spring and split where the meanings split. Color appears midstream because concept directions get written in the middle layers. Near the mouth the rivers turn once more, as the model starts preparing its next word. Steering acts like wind: one vector bends every river the same way.

Lens PCA (fit once, unsteered) · color: valence–honesty

Cat. 5Control

Two Clouds and an Arrow

140 contrastive pairs at layer 8, plus one new prompt being steered

This is contrastive activation addition, drawn literally. Each faint thread joins two prompts that differ only in sentiment. The threads run nearly parallel, and their average is the arrow: mean(joy) minus mean(grief). The white point is a new prompt about a funeral. Drag the slider to add the arrow to it. Past the joy cloud it leaves the region where real activations live.

Lens x: the steering vector · y: top PC orthogonal to it

Cat. 6Control

The Coherence Cliff

One prompt, seven steering strengths, final-layer residual of the last token

Prompt: “I walked home after the funeral and”, steered toward joy at layer 8. The color follows the valence the steering adds. The shape comes from the rest of the vector, the part steering is not supposed to touch, and it holds steady while the tint warms. Then the added vector overwhelms the residual, the form fractures, and at that point the text breaks down into repetition. The continuations are written for illustration, not sampled.

Lens color: valence–honesty · form: fingerprint of the off-steering residual · fracture: how far that residual has moved

Cat. 7Control · live

Growth Rings

One ring per token, drawn as the model reads your prompt and then replies

A mock-up of the live instrument. Each token adds a ring outward, the way a tree adds a year. A ring's wobble is a fingerprint of that token's final residual, and its color is the shared lens. Turn the knob while it reads: rings drawn after the turn are steered, and the reply's tone is set by where the knob sits when your prompt ends. Push past ±9 and the rings start to tear. The replies are canned stand-ins for generation.

Lens form: 24 fixed random projections · color: valence–honesty

About the simulation

No real network produced these vectors. Each token starts as an identity vector, and each of 26 layers adds a write made of six concept components (fixed orthonormal directions), a shared depth drift, noise, and, in the late layers, a next-token direction. Write size grows with depth. Dimension 1570 accumulates a massive activation, and the BOS token holds an attention-sink spike. Steering adds α × 0.09 × ‖h‖ along the valence direction at layer 8. Above α ≈ 7 the simulated writes turn noisy, which stands in for incoherence.

To make these real, replace run() with hooks on a model such as Gemma-2-2B and keep the lenses: fit the PCA on a corpus, take the concept directions from contrastive pairs or probes, and stream per-token activations to the canvas for the live piece.
